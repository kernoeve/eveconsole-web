01

One console for the tools you already juggle

EVE Console pulls your characters' and corporation's data from EVE's ESI API into a local SQLite database on your PC, and turns it into the tools you'd otherwise spread across a handful of separate apps and spreadsheets — market pricing, industry planning, wealth tracking, and corp oversight. It refreshes in the background while it runs, and your data never leaves your machine.

Free & open source Your data stays on your device Background ESI sync Auto-updates Optional AI agent
02

What's inside

Market
Pricing & build costs

Per-item prices from the markets you define, with outlier filtering and build-cost floors — plus a computed build cost for every craftable item.

Industry
Production Calculator

Plan jobs end to end: materials, multi-level builds, ME levels, and profit against your real structures and rigs.

Trade
Opportunities

Compare two markets and surface profitable hauls, ranked by ISK and by cargo volume.

Finance
Wealth tracking

Net worth over time, plus a categorized breakdown of where your ISK comes from and goes.

Corp
Activity & oversight

Ratting, industry and mining tax, donations, killmails, projects, and Top 10 leaderboards.

Browse
Items, assets, contracts

Every item in the game with attributes, orders and price history — and search across all your assets and contracts.

Assistant
AI agent (Eden)

An optional companion that can query your local data to answer what the UI doesn't. Inactive until you set it up.

Upkeep
Stays current

Installed builds check for updates and prompt you in-app — no manual re-downloading.
